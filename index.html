Vistra's premise

Prior authorization is a decision record before it is a workflow. It should be explainable to the member, the clinician, and the regulator.

The argument

Payers do not need another black box that promises speed. They need decisions whose criteria, evidence, and handoffs can be inspected after the fact.

States are setting expectations for deterministic criteria, human review, and appeal trails in AI-assisted prior authorization. The audit is not an edge case. It is part of the product.

Vistra is a career artifact built from the payer's chair: a platform concept that treats the evidence trail as the work.

How it works

Separate the reading from the ruling.

The language model is constrained to intake and extraction with citations. A deterministic rules engine applies the real public CMS LCD criteria. When the record is incomplete or uncertain, the platform does not guess.

01 / INTAKE

Extract with citations

Clinical documents become traceable facts. The source remains attached to every assertion.

02 / DECIDE

Apply published criteria

Deterministic rules encode relevant public CMS LCD policy criteria and produce the decision state.

03 / ESCALATE

UNKNOWN means pend

Ambiguity routes to human review with the evidence trail intact. It never becomes an invented certainty.
