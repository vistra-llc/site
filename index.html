Payer OS / healthcare

Deterministic, auditable prior authorization

Separate the reading from the ruling.

The language model is constrained to intake and extraction with citations. A deterministic rules engine applies relevant public CMS LCD criteria. When the record is incomplete or uncertain, the platform does not guess.

01 / INTAKE

Extract with citations

Clinical documents become traceable facts. The source remains attached to every assertion.

02 / DECIDE

Apply published criteria

Deterministic rules encode relevant public CMS LCD policy criteria and produce the decision state.

03 / ESCALATE

UNKNOWN means pend

Ambiguity routes to human review with the evidence trail intact. It never becomes invented certainty.

Payer OS / proof

Synthetic labeled golden set / offline reference path

Evidence before a score.

The current offline reference-path evaluation produced 24 exact pipeline matches across 24 synthetic labeled cases. An exact match means the stored reference facts, citations, criterion statuses, and deterministic decision matched the expected record.

24 / 24Exact offline pipeline matches
100%Deterministic decision accuracy
0Failures in the defined taxonomy

Failure taxonomy: schema error, citation error, missing fact, unexpected fact, value mismatch, criterion-status mismatch, decision mismatch, and runtime error. These offline results validate the deterministic reference path only; they do not measure live document extraction, clinical quality, or real-world coverage outcomes.

AI Newton / markets

Stock scanning and trading intelligence

Turn a noisy market into a structured research routine.

AI Newton brings market context, strategy-based signals, and tracked outcomes into one research workspace. It supports analysis and discovery; it does not connect to a brokerage or execute trades.

01

Daily briefings

Summarizes market tone, risk context, participation, and notable setups for the selected trading day.

02

Strategy signals

Surfaces Newton and pattern-based setups with entry, stop, status, and position context.

03

Plain-English discovery

Translates natural-language screening questions into structured stock discovery with transparent supporting metrics.

04

Earnings-aware context

Places upcoming and recent earnings information alongside signals so event risk remains visible.

05

Performance tracking

Tracks open and closed strategy positions, returns, and exposure for review—not as a promise of future results.

The safeguards

Healthcare demonstration

Payer OS

Payer OS content uses synthetic data and public policy sources for demonstration and public-policy discussion. It does not provide clinical advice, legal advice, or coverage determinations, and it does not replace qualified human review.

Financial information

AI Newton

AI Newton provides research and educational information, not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell securities. It is not a broker-dealer, does not execute trades, and does not provide automated trading. Markets involve risk; tracked results do not guarantee future performance.
